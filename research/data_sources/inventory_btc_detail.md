## Findings that change Git

1. **No free primary BTC venue is licence-clean for PMV** (S unless marked).
   - **Kraken.** Its API notice requires "prior permission" for "any non-personal commercial use of data from publicly accessible endpoints, such as market data" (contact `marketdata@kraken.com`).
   - **Coinbase.** By accessing the Exchange Market Data API, you agree to its Market Data Terms of Use (FS). Those terms limit use to "personal or research purposes" and bar redistribution of Market Data or derived works. Per Tardis's summary, Section 3 bars AI/ML development, training, validation, or benchmarking without written consent (S).
   - **CME.** The June 2026 DS-45 licence update says "use of Information for any machine learning (ML), artificial intelligence (AI) or large language model (LLM) purposes is strictly prohibited". Whether that reaches ILA-licensed historical data is UNRESOLVED (S). CME also markets its BigQuery data for "BigQuery Machine Learning and Vertex AI" (S), a CONFLICT the owner must have CME resolve in writing.
   - **Binance Vision.** Non-commercial; prohibits live-trading use (G).
2. **Tardis.dev reportedly has the most favourable licence found** (S; docs host refused; not verified). Clause 9.4 reportedly makes downloaded data a perpetual licence that survives termination. Clause 9.5 allows internal, task-specific quantitative models for research, strategy development, backtesting, trading execution, and risk. Coinbase data inside Tardis carries Coinbase's own terms, and is sold only in the "All Exchanges" plan.
3. **The BRTI constituent list changed.** itBit was suspended 2026-07-27 and removed 2026-08-27. Current constituents (S): Bitstamp, Coinbase, Gemini, Kraken, LMAX Digital, Bullish, Crypto.com. A point-in-time list is required.
4. **Doc conflict.** Kraken's developer guide says "Kraken does not provide a bulk historical data dump or websocket replay service" (FS). Support articles describe a full downloadable trade archive (S). This must be resolved before relying on the archive.
5. **Kaiko acquired Amberdata** (June 2026, S).

## Data Scientist escalations (advisory)

- **E-B09-BTC-1 (licence, high).** Coinbase Market Data Terms appear to bar ML training without written consent (S). Batch 3 treated Coinbase as the core feature venue. Proposal: make Coinbase-derived training data conditional on written consent, and prefer Kraken, Bitstamp, and Tardis non-Coinbase data meanwhile. Optional now; blocking before any Coinbase-trained model.
- **E-B09-BTC-2 (licence, high).** The CME DS-45 ML prohibition (S) extends E-U4. Obtain CME's written scope ruling before any Databento or DataMine data, including BRTI via DataMine, trains a model.
- **E-B09-BTC-3 (doc conflict).** The Kraken developer guide denies a bulk dump that the support articles describe. The owner should obtain copies of the support articles.
