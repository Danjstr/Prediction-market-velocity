# B06: Statistician review of `docs/release_tool_design.md` (H-2 per-row release tool, proposed design)

**Reviewer:** development Statistician (`claude-opus-5-5`). This is a **development self-review, not an independent audit.** The development Statistician co-authored `docs/phase2.md` Policies 2 to 5 (the split-isolation and dependence design that R-6 and R-7 implement), so this review cannot stand in for the Gate 2 Independent Statistical Auditor review of those components (H-6).

**Scope:** read-only review of the uncommitted `docs/release_tool_design.md` against:
- `docs/decisions/2026-10-16_phase1_batch4_acquisition_safety_decisions.md` (H-1, H-2, H-6, E-B4-7, and the pre-T0 checklist);
- `docs/phase2.md` (Policies 2 and 3, `I(k)`, `outcome_cell_id`, `Δ_label`, §4 timestamp semantics, §12, §15, §16);
- `docs/protected_information.md` (S-4, guard prerequisites, the "Batch 4" paragraph);
- `research/historical_data/red_team_review.md` (RT-B3-02, RT-B3-05, RT-B3-14);
- START_HERE.md (Evaluation Cycles, pre-T0 checklist P-1 to P-4);
- CLAUDE.md (Holdout Protection, Protected Information Rule);

No real or protected market observation was inspected. No network was used.

## Summary

- Deferring the **code** is correct. RT-B3-05, which H-2 adopts by reference, puts E-U1 and the shared catalog before the tool exists. Two of the three stated reasons, however, are reasons not to **release**, not reasons not to **build**.
- R-1 to R-12 match their sources almost word for word. Nothing is invented. They do **omit** one approved condition: H-1's requirement that the label's own observation or receipt time precede `T0 − m`.
- R-6 is **not sufficient** as a release condition. The main gaps are:
  1. no interval semantics for row times (aggregates, candles, coarse precision, block time);
  2. per-contract eligibility rather than eligibility by dependence unit (`outcome_cell_id`);
  3. no check that the registered `m` satisfies the Policy 2 and Policy 3 preconditions on which R-6's safety depends (including the cap that `m` puts on feature lookback);
  4. derived artifacts and file-level metadata are not covered;
  5. a family-dependent reading of "equivalent observable" would let a misclassified post-boundary field through.
- The prerequisite chain treats step 7 as optional. Under the current records it is not: while guard prerequisites 1, 3, 7, 8, and 9 stay unresolved, the guard refuses everything, so no release can happen.

**Severity counts:** 1 BLOCKING, 7 MAJOR, 7 MINOR. Severities apply to the design as a specification. No code exists, so nothing has leaked.

## Answers to the three questions

**Q1: are R-1 to R-12 faithful, and is R-6 correct and sufficient?**

R-1 to R-5 and R-8 to R-10 faithfully restate H-2, RT-B3-02, RT-B3-05, and S-4. R-6 restates the RT-B3-02 rule ("its contract's `I(k)` ends before `B − m`") correctly as Policy 2 development-eligibility. The **lower** end of `I(k)` (`first_decision − max_lookback`) does not belong in the development-side release test:
- a development-eligible contract's whole `I(k)` lies before `B − m` by construction;
- a released row's own lookback points backwards, away from the boundary.

The lower end matters on the **protected** side. A protected-eligible contract (`first_decision ≥ B`) has `I(k)` disjoint from every released row only if `max_lookback ≤ m`. That is the Policy 3 lower bound on `m`. R-6 is safe only if that bound, and `m + Δ_label ≥ G`, hold for the registered cycle and keep holding. The design neither checks nor states this (B6S-4).

What is missing:
- the H-1 label-receipt condition (B6S-2);
- interval-end row-time semantics (B6S-1);
- cell-level dependence (B6S-3);
- as-of checks on fetches between `B − m` and `T0`, and on revisions (B6S-5);
- derived artifacts (B6S-6);
- family-blind time cutting (B6S-8).

Cross-venue near-copies are handled correctly in principle: they must be catalogued (E-U1, RT-B3-05/14), and an unknown source fails closed (R-5). Their **split** consistency, however, depends on B6S-3.

**Q2: is the deferral reasoning correct, or overly conservative?**

- **Reason 1 (E-U1 first)** is supported. RT-B3-05's fix says "before the release tool exists", and H-2 adopts the RT-B3-05 catalog. I do not recommend changing it: the owner decision on E-U1 is cheap, and building against a placeholder catalog is the retrofit risk RT-B3-02 warns about.
- **Reason 2 (P-3 first)** is **not** supported as a gate on the tool. Under H-2 and R-6 the tool cuts every row and every time-bearing field at `B − m`, whatever its family. The P-3 "near-deterministic" criterion therefore never changes what the tool releases for a cycle. It governs:
  - the guard's allow and refuse decisions for non-equivalent families during the protected interval;
  - O-9 and E-B4-6 embargo granularity;
  - any future decision to release post-boundary non-equivalent data (for example under P-4). That would itself need an owner change to H-2.

  RT-B3-05 places P-3 "before the first release", not before the tool. P-3 is already forced before the first release, because it must precede cycle registration (step 6). Putting it at step 2 needlessly serializes the critical path. The B06 recovery charter also asked for implementation "where permitted", and nothing in the records forbids implementation after E-U1 and the catalog.
- **Reason 3 (no boundary yet)** is correct as a bar on **release** (RT-B3-02 item 1; START_HERE.md Evaluation Cycles). It is not a bar on building or synthetic testing. The tool reads `B` and `m` from the registry, and synthetic registries exist for exactly that purpose.

None of this requires a change to an approved requirement: these are corrections to a proposed design. The one optional escalation, B6S-14, is in CLAUDE.md format below.

**Q3: is the prerequisite chain ordering sound?** Partly. Three changes are needed:
- step 2 should run in parallel with steps 3 to 5 and gate step 6, not step 3 (B6S-8);
- step 7, or resolving guard prerequisites 1, 3, 7, 8, and 9 in the guard, is **mandatory** before the first release (B6S-7);
- step 6 omits other items that are due before the first real protected scope or release: guard prerequisites 4 and 6, the S-4 binding to the holdout log, and the committed F-09 denies (B6S-7).

## Escalation (CLAUDE.md format), for B6S-14

> I recommend changing the current requirement because the first release is irreversible, and the only statistical review scheduled before it is a self-review.

- **Current requirement:** H-6 requires an independent statistical audit of the dependence and split design at Gate 2. H-2 requires Reliability / Red-Team review of the release tool before any release. No independent **statistical** review of the release eligibility rule is required before the first release.
- **Concern:** R-6 and R-7 implement Policy 2 and Policy 3, which the development Statistician co-authored. An error in the eligibility rule (for example B6S-1 to B6S-5) permanently converts the affected periods into development exposure. That cannot be undone and may constrain or contaminate the cycle. The Gate 2 audit would detect such an error only after the harm.
- **Proposed alternative:** before the first release (step 6b), the Independent Statistical Auditor reviews R-3 to R-7 and R-13, and the step 4 fault-injection results, against Policy 2, Policy 3, and H-1. That review is recorded and later cited by the Gate 2 audit.
- **Expected benefit:** catches irreversible eligibility errors before they happen. It is also cheap: the review is of a short specification and its synthetic tests.
- **Risks and tradeoffs:** one extra review before the first release; a slight delay to the first development data.
- **Classification:** optional, strongly recommended. Not blocking under the current requirements.

## Verdict

**REVISE before adoption as the reference specification.** The deferral of the code is correct and should stand, resting primarily on E-U1 and the shared catalog. Before this document is committed as the property list against which step 4 is built and step 5 reviewed:
- **B6S-1 (BLOCKING)** must be fixed;
- **B6S-2 to B6S-8 (MAJOR)** should be fixed.

The MINOR findings can be applied at the same time. No approved requirement needs to change, apart from the optional B6S-14 escalation. The development Statistician's agreement with the revised R-6 and R-7 is development self-review only, not independent validation.
